Leave me a message

From Drew

If you worked a fire lookout, or someone in your family did, or if you’ve camped high or climbed at night and seen something you couldn’t explain, I’d really like to hear about it.

You can record a message right here, up to three minutes long. Take your time. It doesn’t have to be polished, and it doesn’t have to make sense. Some of the best things in my dad’s logbooks don’t make sense either.

Please leave out other people’s full names. If a story belongs to someone else, tell me about it and I’ll ask whether they’d like to talk.
