The other time I drove up Baker Lake Road

Drew Silva, November 1, 2026

Before I go up to Baker for the first real entry, I should admit that I’ve been up Baker Lake Road once before. It was in my twenties, with a few friends, and we never made it as far as the lake. We were going to the hot spring.

It’s up a rough Forest Service road off Baker Lake Road, and then a short walk in through the trees. You smell it before you see it. The sulfur is strong, the rotten-egg kind, and it gets into your hair and your towel and rides home with you. The water comes up out of vents in the ground, and it’s good and hot right near them and cooler the farther out you sit. There isn’t much to it, really, just a few pools in the mud and rock with the forest standing all around.

We weren’t the only ones there. A few old hippies were already soaking when we walked up, gray beards and all, and they looked like they’d been coming to that spring since before any of us were born. They were very friendly. We were all wearing bathing suits. They were not.

I’ve since read that this is more or less the custom there, so I think we were the ones who were dressed wrong.

Because I can’t leave anything alone, I looked up its history this week. It used to be called Morovitz Hot Springs, a name that goes back to the 1890s. In the sixties and seventies a local climbing club built a soaking tub there, and in 1978 the Forest Service took it out and let the place go back to the way it was. If you go, the road in is rough and potholed, and it’s usually snowed in from late fall until spring, so check with the Forest Service before you drive up.

Back then I didn’t know anything about fire lookouts, and I’d never heard of Sauk Mountain. I’m going back up that road soon, and this time I’m going all the way to the lake. I’ll probably stop at the spring on the way home, and I’ll bring a bathing suit.

Drew

Source: Baker Hot Springs, Washington, Hot Springs Guides, updated September 21, 2026.
